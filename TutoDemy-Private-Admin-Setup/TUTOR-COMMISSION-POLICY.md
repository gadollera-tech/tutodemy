# TutoDemy Tutor Commission Policy

## Commission tiers

| Tier | Qualification | Platform commission | Tutor net share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Founding Tutor | Administrator-approved founding eligibility; first 20 completed sessions | 10% | 90% |
| Regular Tutor | Default tier after the founding benefit or when no reduced tier applies | 15% | 85% |
| High-Volume Tutor | At least 100 completed sessions, rating of at least 4.5/5, and good standing | 12% | 88% |
| Top-Rated Tutor | At least 50 completed sessions, rating of at least 4.8/5, at least 20 verified reviews, cancellation rate not above 5%, and good standing | 12% | 88% |

When both High-Volume and Top-Rated conditions apply, the commission remains 12%.

## What counts as a completed session

A booking counts toward a milestone only when:

- the learner's payment has been verified;
- the tutor accepted the booking;
- the scheduled tutoring service was delivered;
- the tutor marked the session delivered;
- an administrator marked the booking completed; and
- no active refund, charge dispute, or confirmed no-show invalidates the session.

Rejected, unpaid, cancelled, refunded, disputed, and undelivered bookings do not count.

## Founding Tutor benefit

Founding status is not automatically granted to every tutor. An administrator must mark the tutor as founding-eligible according to the launch campaign rules.

The 10% commission applies only to the first 20 completed sessions. The tutor then moves to the regular 15% rate unless the tutor qualifies for a 12% performance tier.

## Performance tiers

The system may calculate eligibility after completed bookings, but TutoDemy may place a tutor back on the regular tier when the account has:

- unresolved policy violations;
- suspected fraudulent activity;
- repeated tutor-caused cancellations or no-shows;
- manipulated reviews;
- unresolved learner complaints; or
- suspended or restricted status.

A tutor may regain a reduced tier after the account returns to good standing and the administrator confirms eligibility.

## Commission calculation

The commission snapshot is calculated from the booking's agreed gross tutoring amount when the booking is completed.

```text
platform commission = gross booking amount × commission rate
tutor net earnings = gross booking amount − platform commission
```

Example for a ₱500 completed booking:

| Tier | Commission | Tutor earnings |
|---|---:|---:|
| Founding Tutor | ₱50 | ₱450 |
| Regular Tutor | ₱75 | ₱425 |
| High-Volume or Top-Rated | ₱60 | ₱440 |

## Payment processing

The current release uses administrator-confirmed manual payment handling. Payment-processor charges, payout charges, taxes, refunds, and other deductions are not automatically calculated by this website and must be covered by the separately published operating policy.

## Transparency

The tutor must be able to see the gross amount, commission rate, commission amount, and net tutor earnings recorded for each completed booking.
