TutoDemy Learning PH — Learner Progress & Community Update

Public website changes:
- redesigned learner dashboard with preparation score, estimated percentile, streaks, weekly activity, and topic coverage
- daily Most Active leaderboard and Overall Preparation leaderboard
- weekly Top 3 and rolling Hall of Fame
- opt-in leaderboard privacy controls and learner display names
- profile-picture upload through Supabase Storage plus six preset avatar styles
- profile avatar shown on profile, dashboard, and updated account chip
- DOST-SEI attempts now contribute to the main learner dashboard and cloud progress
- privacy notice updated for rankings, percentiles, avatars, and learning metrics

Required private setup:
Run LEARNER-PROGRESS-COMMUNITY-UPGRADE.sql from the separate private setup package in Supabase SQL Editor before testing the new community features.

Rules implemented:
- streak day: at least 10 completed questions in Philippine time
- percentile eligibility: at least 30 completed questions and at least 5 eligible learners in the same preparation track
- leaderboard participation: off by default and must be enabled in Profile
- public leaderboard output: display name, avatar, track, and limited learning totals only
- exact email, school, city, province, and account identifiers are not returned in ranking results
- preparation metrics are learning insights, not examination-result predictions

Deployment:
Upload the public files only. Never upload the private SQL package to GitHub.
