TutoDemy Learning PH — Phase 4B: Private Learner Percentiles

Adds private overall-preparation, accuracy, 28-day activity, and subject percentiles to the Phase 4A student dashboard. Comparisons require at least 20 eligible learners. Overall/accuracy require 30 completed questions; activity requires 20 questions in 28 days; subject comparisons require 20 questions in the same review bank and preparation track.

Percentiles are relative learning indicators, not official grades or academic ranks. No names, schools, emails, locations, profiles, or answers from other learners are returned.

INSTALLATION
1. Confirm Phase 4A SQL is installed.
2. Run STUDENT-PERCENTILES-PHASE-4B.sql privately in Supabase.
3. Upload this website or the public patch.
4. Commit, push, and hard-refresh dashboard.html once.

Never upload the SQL/private setup package to GitHub.
