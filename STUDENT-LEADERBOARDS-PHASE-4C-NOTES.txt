Private database setup
- STUDENT-LEADERBOARDS-PHASE-4C.sql must be run separately in Supabase SQL Editor.
- Do NOT upload SQL setup files to GitHub/public hosting.

Leaderboard rules
- Participation is opt-in.
- Full names, schools, emails, answers and user IDs are never returned.
- Blank aliases become stable generated Study Owl aliases.
- Minimum 20 counted questions/week to qualify.
- Minimum 3 eligible opt-in learners before public ranks appear.
- Ranking uses capped questions + consistency + small accuracy bonus.
- At most 75 questions/day and 350 questions/week count toward points.
- Current week and previous week are available.
- Subject/review-bank filter is supported.
- The signed-in learner sees their own rank even outside the Top 10 when eligible.
