THE STORY

A tutoring practice growing into an academic support platform.

TutoDemy Learning PH is built from firsthand tutoring experience and a long-term goal: make quality explanations, practice materials, and review support easier to access.

2021The tutoring journey began
WHY TUTODEMY EXISTS

Students usually need more than answers.

They need someone to find the point of confusion, explain it without judgment, provide the right practice, and help them see progress.

TutoDemy brings together tutor matching, organized reviewers, entrance-exam preparation, and independent study tools.

LEARNING PRINCIPLES

What every TutoDemy resource should do.

01

Begin with clarity

Define the goal, prerequisite knowledge, and expected output.

02

Teach the “why”

Explain reasoning so students can transfer learning.

03

Practice deliberately

Use questions that reveal thinking and provide feedback.

04

Respect the learner

Use patient language, realistic pacing, and helpful feedback.

05

Verify before publishing

Check solutions, answer keys, units, diagrams, and references.

06

Improve continuously

Use learner questions and performance to refine materials.
