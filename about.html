ABOUT TUTODEMY

About TutoDemy

TutoDemy combines exam practice, reviewers, tutor booking, and progress tools.

THE VISION

What TutoDemy does

Tutees can practice, review, track progress, and book tutors in one account.

The platform includes tutee accounts, tutor applications, approved profiles, bookings, messages, and progress tracking.

WHAT IS ALREADY WORKING

Current features

Searchable reviewer modules with domain-linked practice.

NOW

Tutor directory

Role-based tutor onboarding, private verification uploads, admin approval, public profiles, schedule requests, and verified reviews.
