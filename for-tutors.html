HOW TO JOIN

Application process

You may save a draft before submitting. Your public tutor profile appears only after admin approval.

Start your application

  1. Create a tutor account

    Confirm your email, then continue to the tutor application.

  2. Complete your profile

    Add teaching details, location, availability, public rate, and private payout information.

  3. Upload verification documents

    Supporting documents stay private and are reviewed only by authorized administrators.

  4. Submit for approval

    Once approved, your public profile can appear in the tutor directory.
