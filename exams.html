THE REVIEW CYCLE

Five phases that make preparation manageable.

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    Take a baseline test and identify topic, speed, and accuracy gaps.

  2. 2

    Rebuild

    Strengthen foundations before difficult drills.

  3. 3

    Drill

    Practice by topic with immediate error review.

  4. 4

    Simulate

    Use timed sets and realistic mock exams.

  5. 5

    Refine

    Prioritize recurring weak areas before test day.
