# Source Basis

The final Waves 1–4 bank follows the broad preparation blueprint developed from the uploaded source set.

Source-supported organization includes:

- Verbal Reasoning
- Non-Verbal Reasoning
- English
- Science
- Mathematics
- Mechanical-Technical
- optional unscored Self-Inventory in official-style preparation materials

The private references were used for broad coverage and style analysis. They were not reproduced in the package.

The final bank uses original content and TutoDemy training specifications where the private sources did not support an exact public item count, scoring rule, or time limit.
