# Content Review Checklist — Completed

This checklist records the review standard completed before the bank was approved for distribution. Reuse it for future revisions.

## Every question

- [ ] Stem has one clear intended interpretation.
- [ ] Exactly one answer is best.
- [ ] Distractors are plausible but demonstrably incorrect.
- [ ] Key still matches after any wording edit.
- [ ] Rationale explains why the key is correct.
- [ ] Units, notation, capitalization, and punctuation are consistent.
- [ ] Content does not copy protected source wording or figures.
- [ ] Difficulty label is reasonable.
- [ ] Free or Premium label is intentional.

## Mathematics and Science

- [ ] Calculation independently repeated.
- [ ] Significant figures and units checked.
- [ ] Assumptions are stated when needed.
- [ ] Diagram values match the stem and key.
- [ ] No hidden calculator dependency in no-calculator items.

## English and Verbal Reasoning

- [ ] Grammar rule independently verified.
- [ ] Vocabulary meaning fits the exact context.
- [ ] Passage question is supported by the passage alone.
- [ ] Logical conclusion is necessary, not merely possible.
- [ ] Parallel choices use comparable grammatical forms.

## Non-Verbal and Mechanical Visuals

- [ ] Figure renders correctly on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Correct option is visibly distinct and valid.
- [ ] Distractors do not accidentally satisfy the rule.
- [ ] Cube net folds correctly.
- [ ] Paper-folding punch locations are unambiguous.
- [ ] Route coordinates and arrows are readable.
- [ ] Mechanical arrows, labels, and component connections are correct.

## Final approval record

Record reviewer name, date, subject, decision, and any revision notes outside the public question data.
