# Strict Mock Specification

## Training form

- 200 unique questions
- six separately timed sections
- four choices per scored question
- no movement to a later section before submission
- no return to a submitted section
- automatic section submission when time reaches zero
- randomized questions and answer choices
- exact seeded resume after refresh
- flags and navigation within the active section
- explanations only after completing the full attempt

## Allocation

| Section | Items | Training time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Verbal Reasoning | 20 | 20 min |
| Non-Verbal Reasoning | 30 | 35 min |
| English | 30 | 35 min |
| Science | 45 | 60 min |
| Mathematics | 45 | 60 min |
| Mechanical-Technical | 30 | 30 min |
| **Total** | **200** | **240 min** |

Science:

- 12 Biology
- 11 Chemistry
- 12 Physics
- 10 Earth Science

The allocation is a TutoDemy training default, not a claim about confidential or current official DOST-SEI item counts or time limits.

## Scoring limits

The prototype reports raw score and accuracy. It does not fabricate:

- an official passing score
- qualification probability
- percentile rank
- official standardized score
