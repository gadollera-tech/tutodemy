TUTOR MARKETPLACE POLICY

Clear commission rules for every completed booking.

TutoDemy charges commission only on successfully completed and paid tutoring sessions. Profile creation, pending applications, rejected requests, unpaid cancellations, and refunded bookings do not generate commission.

COMMISSION TIERS

Founding, regular, and achievement-based rates

10%

Founding Tutor

Available only to tutors specifically marked as Founding-eligible by TutoDemy administration. The 10% rate applies to their first 20 completed sessions.

15%

Regular Tutor

The standard platform commission after the founding benefit ends or when a tutor does not yet qualify for an achievement-based rate.

12%

High-Volume Tutor

At least 100 completed sessions, a rating of at least 4.5/5, good account standing, and no unresolved serious violations.

12%

Top-Rated Tutor

At least 50 completed sessions, rating of at least 4.8/5, at least 20 verified reviews, cancellation rate not above 5%, and good standing.

The database snapshots the applicable tier and rate when an authorized administrator completes a paid, delivered booking.

COMPLETED SESSION

When a booking counts

A session counts toward milestones only when the learner has paid, the tutor accepted the booking, the scheduled lesson occurred, the tutor marked it delivered, and TutoDemy administration marked it completed. An active refund, dispute, confirmed tutor no-show, or fraudulent transaction prevents completion.

BOOKING FLOW

Request before payment

  1. The learner chooses an approved tutor and proposes a schedule.
  2. The tutor accepts or declines the request.
  3. Payment is handled through the current TutoDemy operational process and manually confirmed by an administrator.
  4. The tutor delivers the session and marks it delivered.
  5. An administrator completes the booking and records the commission split.
CANCELLATIONS

No commission on unpaid or fully refunded sessions

Learners may cancel unpaid requested or accepted bookings from their dashboard. Paid cancellations, late cancellations, no-shows, partial refunds, and rescheduling require admin review under the operational cancellation terms disclosed before payment.

REVIEWS AND ACCOUNT STANDING

Only completed bookings may produce public reviews

Each completed booking may receive one learner review. TutoDemy may hide abusive, fraudulent, irrelevant, or privacy-violating reviews. Tutors can lose an achievement-based 12% tier if ratings, cancellation history, account standing, or conduct requirements are no longer met.

PAYMENTS AND PAYOUTS

Manual confirmation until automated marketplace payments are connected

The current site records payment references and commission calculations after manual verification. It does not automatically collect, split, hold, or transfer funds. TutoDemy should connect a compliant marketplace payment provider and publish detailed payout, refund, tax, and dispute terms before enabling automated money movement.
