Tutor Booking Guidelines

Rules for tutor approval, bookings, payments, cancellations, reviews, and conduct.

TUTOR FEES

Tutor compensation

Learners and parents see the tutor's published session rate and the total booking amount. TutoDemy's platform-fee percentages, tutor tier, net earnings, and settlement records are private account information shown only to the tutor and authorized administrators.

Registered tutors receive the applicable fee schedule in their private tutor dashboard. The rate applied to a completed booking is recorded in the tutor's private earnings ledger.

COMPLETED SESSION

Completed bookings

A session is recorded as completed only after the tutor accepts the request, payment is confirmed, the scheduled lesson occurs, the tutor marks it delivered, and TutoDemy administration closes the booking. Active refunds, disputes, confirmed no-shows, or fraudulent transactions may prevent completion.

BOOKING FLOW

Booking and payment

  1. The learner chooses an approved tutor and proposes a schedule.
  2. The tutor accepts or declines the request.
  3. TutoDemy provides the applicable payment instructions and confirms the transaction.
  4. The tutor delivers the session and marks it delivered.
  5. TutoDemy closes the booking and enables the completed-session record and review option.
MESSAGES AND ONLINE LESSONS

Messages

Messaging opens after the tutor accepts the booking. Learners, parents, and tutors should use the official thread for schedule confirmation, lesson preparation, and private Google Meet or Zoom links. Personal social-media accounts and phone numbers are optional and should not be required. Users may report a conversation, and authorized administrators may review messages for safeguarding, support, disputes, or policy enforcement.

Tutors must not request passwords, wallet PINs, complete banking details, unnecessary home-address information, or private communication with a minor that excludes the responsible parent or guardian.

CANCELLATIONS

Cancellations

Paid cancellations, late cancellations, no-shows, partial refunds, and rescheduling are reviewed according to the terms disclosed for the booking. TutoDemy may request supporting information from either party before resolving a dispute.

REVIEWS AND ACCOUNT STANDING

Only completed bookings may produce verified reviews

Each completed booking may receive one learner review. TutoDemy may hide abusive, fraudulent, irrelevant, or privacy-violating content. Tutor visibility and account standing may be affected by repeated cancellations, no-shows, policy violations, misleading information, or substantiated complaints.

PAYMENTS

Payment is confirmed after tutor acceptance

No payment is collected on the initial booking-request form. Payment instructions are provided only after the tutor accepts the schedule. Learners should use only the official payment method and reference instructions supplied by TutoDemy for that booking.

Weekly tutor payouts

Tutor net earnings are recorded after a paid session is delivered and completed. Eligible balances are processed in a weekly payout batch to the tutor’s private registered GCash destination. Tutors are responsible for keeping the registered name, mobile number, and optional QR code accurate. Processing may be delayed by verification, disputes, weekends, holidays, or service interruptions.
