Privacy Notice

This notice explains how TutoDemy handles account, learning, tutor, booking, and review data.

SECTIONSLearner and parent dataTutor dataBookings and paymentsBooking messagesStorage and accessUser choices
Private verification files

Tutor identity and credential documents are not displayed in public profiles.

LEARNER AND PARENT DATA

Account and learning data

TutoDemy may store email, full name, optional student level, target exam, school, avatar, practice attempts, saved reviewers, unfinished exam sessions, tutor booking requests, learning goals, reviews, and support inquiries.

TUTOR DATA

Tutor profiles and verification

Approved public tutor profiles may show display name, photo, biography, subjects, exam specializations, learner levels, teaching modes, general location, availability, education, credentials summary, experience, session rate, ratings, and completed-session statistics.

Contact details, identity files, credential documents, references, application decisions, administrator notes, payment references, platform-fee information, and tutor earnings records are restricted to the tutor and authorized administrators.

BOOKINGS AND PAYMENT RECORDS

Bookings and payments

Booking records may include the learner and tutor account IDs, proposed schedule, subject, learning goal, teaching mode, general meeting details, session rate, booking amount, payment-confirmation status, status history, and verified review.

TutoDemy does not ask users to place wallet PINs, passwords, complete bank credentials, card numbers, or unnecessary home-address details in booking notes.

BOOKING MESSAGES

Messages and safety

After a tutor accepts a booking, the learner or parent and the assigned tutor may exchange private text messages for scheduling, lesson preparation, and online-meeting links. Authorized TutoDemy administrators may access booking conversations when needed for safeguarding, support, payment verification, policy enforcement, or dispute resolution.

Messages, read timestamps, conversation reports, and related booking identifiers may be retained with the booking record. Users should not send passwords, wallet PINs, complete card or banking details, unnecessary home-address information, or unrelated sensitive documents through messaging.

STORAGE AND ACCESS

Device and account storage

Learning progress may be retained on the current device and synchronized to the user's account when logged in. Access controls limit account and booking data according to user role and booking relationship. Tutor profile photos may be public after approval; verification files remain private.

USER CHOICES

Access, correction, export, and deletion

Users may update information available through their account and may request assistance with access, correction, deletion, tutor-record questions, or review disputes through TutoDemy's official contact channel.

Payment and payout information

Payment receipts, transaction references, payer names, tutor GCash details, payout QR codes, payout records, commission data, and earnings information are private operational records. Access is limited to the relevant account holder and authorized TutoDemy administrators, subject to booking and payout responsibilities. These details are not displayed in public tutor profiles.

LOCATION INSIGHTS

Regional map data

Tutor availability may be summarized from the province shown in an approved public tutor profile. Learners may optionally share a province for anonymous regional demand insights. Learner totals are displayed only in aggregate and only after a minimum group size is reached. TutoDemy does not publish a learner's name, exact address, contact information, or individual map marker.

Users may turn off learner-location sharing or remove their province from their profile. Location information is used to improve regional tutor availability and service planning.
