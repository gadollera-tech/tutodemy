PRIVACY NOTICE

Collect only what the learning and tutoring service needs.

This notice describes the supplied TutoDemy data model. Update the support contact, legal entity, retention periods, and operational procedures before accepting live paid bookings.

SECTIONSLearner and parent dataTutor dataBookings and paymentsStorage and accessUser choices
Private verification files

Government IDs and credential documents must never be placed in a public GitHub repository or public Storage bucket.

LEARNER AND PARENT DATA

Account and learning information

The service may store email, full name, optional student level, target exam, school, avatar URL, practice attempts, saved reviewers, active exam sessions, tutor booking requests, learning goals, reviews, and support inquiries.

TUTOR DATA

Public profile and private verification

Approved public tutor profiles may show display name, photo, biography, subjects, exam specializations, learner levels, teaching modes, general location, availability, education, credentials summary, experience, rate, ratings, and completed-session statistics.

Contact email, identification files, education documents, references, admin notes, payment references, and verification status are restricted to the tutor and authorized administrators through database and Storage access policies.

BOOKINGS AND PAYMENT RECORDS

Scheduling, status, and commission data

Booking records may include the learner and tutor account IDs, proposed schedule, subject, learning goal, mode, general meeting details, rate snapshot, amount, payment-confirmation reference, status history, commission rate, platform commission, tutor net amount, and verified review.

The supplied website does not store card numbers or automatically process money. Do not place full bank-account credentials, wallet PINs, passwords, or unnecessary home-address details in booking notes.

STORAGE AND ACCESS

Local browser, Supabase database, and Storage

Learning progress may be retained in browser storage for resilience and synchronized to Supabase when the user is logged in. Row Level Security limits database access by user role and booking relationship. Tutor profile photos use a public avatar bucket; private verification files use a separate non-public bucket.

USER CHOICES

Access, correction, export, and deletion

Users may edit account and tutor-profile information allowed by their role. Learners can export local learning data. A public launch must provide a verified support channel for cloud-data access, correction, account deletion, tutor-record questions, review disputes, and retention requests.
