TutoDemy Inquiry Messaging
Build: 20260819-inquiry1

NEW FLOW

Student:
Find Tutor
-> Send Inquiry
-> private conversation
-> Request Booking when ready
-> tutor accepts
-> PayMongo payment
-> session

Inquiry:
- No payment required.
- One open inquiry per student/tutor pair.
- Student and tutor can reply while the inquiry is open.
- Either participant can close the inquiry.
- Closed inquiries become read-only.
- If a booking is requested from the inquiry, the inquiry stays open and the booking is linked to it.

Tutor Profile:
- "Send Inquiry" appears before the booking form.
- Booking CTA is "Request Booking".
- When opened from an inquiry, a new booking is linked back to that inquiry when possible.

Messages:
- Inquiry and booking conversations share one Messages inbox.
- Inquiry threads are clearly labeled.
- Student sees "Request Booking" inside an open inquiry.
- Linked inquiries show "View Booking".
- Realtime inquiry messages are supported.
- Inquiry notifications use the existing TutoDemy notification/email/push pipeline.

Admin:
- Inquiry safety reports appear together with booking conversation reports.

PAYMENT
No PayMongo logic changed.
A student cannot pay from an inquiry.
PayMongo still appears only after an accepted booking.
